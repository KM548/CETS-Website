CETS 2024 - 2025 General Financial Report

In line with the Council of Engineering and Technology Students' (CETS) commitment to transparency and accountability, the General Financial Report of the previous CETS officers for the academic year 2024–2025 is made accessible on this page for the information and reference of CETS students. This report provides an overview of the organization's financial transactions, including its funds, income, and expenditures throughout the officers' term. By making this report available, CETS aims to promote financial transparency, strengthen students' trust in the organization, and ensure that students are informed about how organizational funds were managed and utilized. All CETS students are encouraged to access and review the report to better understand the organization's financial records and support a culture of responsible leadership and accountability.
